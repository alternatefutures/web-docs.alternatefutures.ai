Alternate Clouds billing has two parts:

- **Subscription** - your plan (billed via Stripe, or funded from your credit
  wallet).
- **Credit wallet** - prepaid credits (USD) that pay for what you actually run.
  Compute usage is metered and debited from the wallet as services run.

## Plans and Trial

| Plan | Price |
|------|-------|
| Monthly | $25 / seat / month |
| Yearly | $240 / seat / year ($20/mo equivalent) |

- Every new account starts a **14-day trial** - no credit card required.
- Signing up with a verified email grants a **$5 signup credit** (wallet-only
  signups can claim it later by verifying an email in Account Settings).
- Inviting team members requires an active paid subscription.
- Subscriptions can be paid by card or funded from your credit wallet.

## Billing Dashboard

Organization owners and admins can manage billing at
**app.alternatefutures.ai → Billing**:

- **Usage** - what your services are consuming and costing
- **Payments** - payment history and credit topups
- **Invoices** - past invoices
- **Plans** - view and change your subscription
- **Credits wallet** - balance and topup (card or crypto)

## CLI Commands

### Check your balance

```bash
acc billing balance
```

### Top up credits

```bash
# Card topup opens the browser checkout
acc billing topup --amount 25

# Crypto topup (defaults: chain base, token USDC)
acc billing topup --crypto --amount 25 --refund-address <your-address>
```

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| `--crypto` | Pay with crypto instead of card |
| `--amount <usd>` | Topup amount in USD |
| `--chain <chain>` | Chain for crypto topup (default: `base`) |
| `--token <token>` | Token for crypto topup (default: `USDC`) |
| `--refund-address <address>` | Refund address for over/under payments |
| `--org <idOrSlug>` | Top up a specific organization |
| `--no-wait` | Don't wait for confirmation |

After a topup, deployments that were suspended for insufficient credits are
resumed automatically.

## Budgets and Spend Controls

Set spend limits per service when creating or deploying:

```bash
acc services create --kind docker --image nginx:latest \
  --budget-monthly 20        # cap this service at $20/month

acc services deploy --stop-hours 8   # auto-stop after 8 hours
```

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| `--spend <mode>` | Spend control mode |
| `--budget-total <usd>` | Lifetime budget cap |
| `--budget-monthly <usd>` | Monthly budget cap |
| `--stop-hours <n>` / `--stop-days <n>` | Auto-stop timer |

## What happens when credits run out

Services are suspended when your wallet cannot cover further usage. Top up to
resume them automatically, or close services you no longer need:

```bash
acc services close
```

<Callout type="info">
Usage is billed hourly per running service. Check `acc services list` for what
is currently running and `acc billing balance` for your remaining credits.
</Callout>
